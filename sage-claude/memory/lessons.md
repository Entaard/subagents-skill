# Sage lessons

The curated lessons a sage run reads whole at Step 2. `/sage-promote` is the only writer, and the repo copy is the source of truth. Contract and cap: `~/.claude/skills/sage-promote/references/memory-contract.md`. Figures are per agent unless stated, and they are this ecosystem's measurements, not vendor facts.

## Bands

- **band-dispatch-floor** — Every dispatch pays a boot floor of about 5k tokens for a scoped saved agent and about 16k for a general-purpose one. The absolute floors are machine-specific, because they include this machine's always-loaded files. Only the ~3× ratio travels. The batching rule is in `decompose.md`. When: sizing a probe, a scout wave, or a fleet of small units. Falsifier: a scoped no-op dispatch that boots at more than twice the floor on an unchanged harness. Runs: a58bd85c, 056e20a8, 21c23a0e.
- **band-frontier-review-lens-prose-corpus-10k-words** — Price one frontier review lens over a prose corpus of up to 10k words at 50–95k; the corpus the reviewer must hold sets the cost, not the size of the change. When: pricing a review lens. Code lenses landed 81–100k. Several documents move it up 2–6× only with size past the word limit or steer rounds; document count alone does not. A fully specified refuter brief moves one round down. Falsifier: three lenses over ≤10k words of prose landing outside the band with no qualifier that explains it. Runs: 42dd56d4, bc00ef0a, 056e20a8.
- **band-web-research-brief-names-its-target-urls** — A research unit whose brief names its target URLs lands at 14–34k; one that must find its own sources lands at 70–120k, so name the URLs. When: briefing a `web-researcher`. Falsifier: URL-named briefs landing above 60k three times. Runs: seed.
- **band-writer-unit-on-a-skill-or-prose-corpus-under-a-write-lease** — A writer thread that edits a skill or prose corpus under a lease lands at 170–250k, and more than 40 findings price it at the top of the band times two. When: pricing a corpus-edit writer. Falsifier: three such threads landing below 100k. Runs: seed.
- **band-any-single-agent-all-classes** — The distribution of 195 agent transcripts on this machine (non-recursive, `wf_*` excluded; nearest-rank p50 164k, p90 410k, max 818k) prices nothing on its own; price from a same-shape `runs.log` row instead. When: tempted to price a unit from the global spread. Falsifier: none; it is a warning, not a price. Runs: cf94c4c4.

## Lessons

- **a-checklist-prices-a-lens-only-when-every-item-settles-in-one-look** — A claim checklist prices a review lens at its band's floor only when every item settles in one look; one item quantified over the corpus ("how many", "everywhere", "all of") costs an open mandate. When: pricing a lens from a checklist. Falsifier: a lens carrying a corpus-quantified item that lands at its band's floor three times. Runs: seed.
- **lesson-teaching-corpus-can-carry-wrong-verification** — When a corpus records its own fact-checking ("verified live" stamps), treat that record as an artifact under review, not as ground truth. When: reviewing a teaching or documentation corpus. Falsifier: three such corpora whose stamps all hold under independent re-checks. Runs: d6ca31ad.
- **lesson-a-precision-fix-adds-claim-surface** — After a passage fails two verify rounds, the next edit removes assertions instead of adding qualifiers, because each added qualifier is new unreviewed text. When: a verify round whose finding is against the previous fix's own sentence. Falsifier: a passage failing two rounds that converges on a third round of added qualifiers. Runs: e4bebf20, 21c23a0e.
- **lesson-whole-file-prose-compression-does-not-converge** — Shrink behaviour-shaping prose by deleting exact-match duplicates and moving conditional sections out, then run regression lenses; a whole-file rewrite paraphrases at equal length. When: a compression plan whose method is "rewrite it shorter". Falsifier: a whole-file compression pass that lands within 10% of its target on the first try. Runs: d4096102, promote-pass-7.
- **lesson-zsh-unquoted-expansion-does-not-word-split** — In zsh an unquoted `$VAR` does not word-split, so a flag string in a variable passes as one argument; build argument arrays, and read stderr before you trust an empty result. When: a zsh command that expands arguments from a variable. Falsifier: zsh splitting an unquoted `$VAR` into separate arguments by default. Runs: e4bebf20, promote-pass-7.
- **lesson-a-table-introducing-sentence-describes-the-table** — Read a sentence that introduces a table back against the rows, not against its subject, because it describes the table. When: writing or reviewing the sentence above a table. Falsifier: two such sentences where the subject reading and the row reading are checked separately and never diverge. Runs: promote-pass-2.
