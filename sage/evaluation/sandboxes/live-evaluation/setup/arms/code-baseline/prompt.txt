Reject oversize frames as soon as the length proves too large without accumulating unbounded headers. Any framing error poisons the decoder: further feed/finish calls raise FrameError until reset clears all state. finish rejects any partial frame, accepts a clean boundary, and does not close the decoder. A feed TypeError does not poison or discard existing state. Payloads are bytes, including commas, colons, NUL and multibyte text. Provide meaningful stdlib tests exercising arbitrary chunk boundaries, malformed/truncated frames, limit enforcement, poisoning and recovery, plus a short usage README. Preserve inputs.
