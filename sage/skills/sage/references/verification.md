# Verification and completion

Read before freezing a substantive candidate and again before closure.

Run the cheapest decisive checks first. Verify the actual acceptance criteria and excellence conditions, not only buildability. Use current primary evidence for load-bearing external claims and preserve recency, counterevidence, and uncertainty.

Substantive or risky work gets an independent reviewer with fresh artifact/request context and no builder rationale. Request separate compliance and quality verdicts, findings with severity, exact evidence, affected criterion, and a falsifiable fix check. One reviewer can cover both axes; additional reviewers need distinct failure modes. No findings is valid, and unexecuted checks remain unobserved. Reviewers do not repair their own findings. Tiny low-risk work may use direct checks without a worker fleet.

For consequential work, give a separate adversarial pass a concrete target: a load-bearing assumption, failure/recovery boundary, counterexample to the claimed behavior, or the root's repairs and completion claim. Reserve this in the plan rather than adding undirected reviewers after agreement. Use a fresh actor for a materially changed design; a still-independent reviewer may recheck a narrow repair with a distinct attack mandate. The adversary seeks disconfirming evidence, not a desired rejection or a quota of findings. Model diversity within the shared allowlist is optional and never substitutes for executable evidence.

For a substantive candidate, record its observed artifact digests and predeclare independent required check obligations using the [versioned bindings](state.md#artifact-and-check-bindings-v1). Each attempt binds the exact obligation revision, current criterion IDs, and all artifacts it assesses. Distinct required checks retain distinct obligation IDs. Inspect actual files and evidence before recording these facts; the helper validates recorded relationships, not filesystem freshness or oracle quality.

The root dispositions every finding from evidence:

- `fixed`: a focused independent check observes the repair;
- `rejected`: contrary evidence resolves the finding;
- `accepted`: an explicit residual limitation, permitted only when it does not leave a blocker or major issue in a completed run.

After fixes, record changed artifact digests and map touched files/interfaces/claims to affected obligations. Rerun those checks and a final integration check against the final freeze; widen coverage when impact is uncertain. A bound finding's repair check must follow the finding and assess the current artifact. A historical passing check or an earlier review cannot certify a changed candidate. For interactive or perceptual artifacts, exercise representative flows and record scoped visual, usability, error-state, and performance observations. Name untested platforms, viewports, inputs, and human judgments.

For a defect, reproduce the triggering condition and check the resulting behavior, not just the implementation shape. Preservation tests can legitimately pass both before and after. For guidance, give an independent agent a realistic request, the changed skill, and minimum raw artifacts without the intended answer; inspect its decisions/deliverable and scope. A single successful scenario supports that scenario only.

Exercise the final artifact through the user's delivery boundary: the installed helper/skill, exported document, actual input flow, or reproducible data transformation. Check the original purpose again. Source tests, reviewer agreement, and plausible prose cannot stand in for an unobserved delivery check.

Additional review rounds need a changed artifact, new evidence, or a distinct unresolved risk. Deduplicate against rejected findings as well as fixed ones. Honor user review caps and finite continuation authority; unresolved major issues keep the outcome incomplete.

Before closure, append typed `unknown` or `untested` evidence for every material task fact or experience check that remains unobserved. Reports render those recorded facts and unknown native identity/effect fields; they do not discover semantics hidden in arbitrary notes or deliverable prose.

Close `completed` only when every criterion has observation evidence and a passed check, effects are reconciled, material findings are dispositioned with no accepted blocker/major, scope is reconciled, and remaining human items are explicit. A required human criterion without observation remains incomplete; use `failed` or `stopped` honestly. Worker reports are claims until the root checks their locators/artifacts.
